About Marvellous

Most people know me as The Realm. I'm a creative media director and a Computer Engineering graduate of the University of Benin, working where media and technology meet.

I founded MarvelC in 2022 to amplify voices and visions through media and tech, and to train young creatives who have talent but lack access. Today I work on content full-time and serve in church media at Grace-Tribe.
